# Review by Expendifii

Turn happy customers into Google reviews - without asking them to do any work.

## The problem

Most happy customers never leave a review. Not because they didn't enjoy their visit, but because leaving a review is annoying - open Google, search your name, find the listing, type something on a small screen. So the only people who bother are usually the upset ones. That's why a business's Google rating often looks worse than the business actually is.

## What this does

You put a QR code on the table, counter, or receipt. A customer scans it and answers three quick taps: what they had, how it was, anything worth mentioning. From those taps, we write a short, natural-sounding draft review in their words. They can edit it if they want, then post it straight to your real Google listing in one tap.

No app to download. No account for the customer to create. It works on any phone with a camera.

## Why a business needs this

- **More reviews, with less effort from customers.** Tapping three buttons is much easier than typing a review from scratch, so more people actually finish it.
- **A fairer rating.** Right now only your angriest or happiest customers bother to review you. This makes it just as easy for everyone in between, so your rating reflects your real average, not just your extremes.
- **You find out what's working and what isn't.** You see which dishes or items get mentioned most, what people complain about, and which days or times are strong or weak - without having to read through hundreds of reviews yourself.
- **It's honest.** Customers write their own words. We never write a review for them and never post on their behalf. Everyone sees the same "post to Google" button, whatever rating they give - we never hide it from an unhappy customer. There are no discounts or rewards for leaving a review, since that breaks Google's rules and can get a listing penalised.
- **Set up once, works forever.** One QR code per location. Print it, place it, done.

## Frequently asked questions

**Does this post fake or paid reviews?**
No. The customer always writes and approves their own words before anything is posted, and we never pay or offer discounts for a review. This keeps your Google listing genuine and safe from penalties.

**Can we choose what customers see?**
You control your menu items and can mark your best ones as "featured" so they show up first, but you cannot pick and choose which reviews go public - every customer sees the same option to post, whatever they rate you.

**What if a customer leaves a bad review?**
Bad reviews still go to Google like any other - that's honest feedback you'd want to know about anyway. But because this makes it easy for every customer to review you, not just the upset ones, one bad review gets balanced out by many fair ones instead of standing alone.

**Do customers need to download anything?**
No. It works entirely in their phone's browser after scanning the QR code.

**How long does setup take?**
A few minutes. You get a QR code as soon as your account is created - print it and put it on your tables.

**What happens if we cancel?**
Your QR code keeps working for 30 days after you cancel, so there's no sudden gap for customers. Your data isn't deleted - if you come back, your history is still there.

**Can we see reports without the paid plan?**
The free tier still shows your QR code and total scan count. Ratings, trends, and deeper reports need Basic or Full.

**Is our data safe?**
Only your business can see your own reports and menu. Customer answers are used only to build their review draft and your analytics - nothing is sold or shared.
