# Review by Expendifii - Pitch & Pricing Deck

## The problem

Most happy customers never leave a review. Not because they didn't enjoy the visit - because reviewing is annoying. Open Google, search the name, find the listing, type something on a phone. So the only people who bother are usually the upset ones. A business's public rating ends up worse than the business actually is.

## The product, in one line

A QR code on the table. The customer taps through three quick questions, gets a short draft written in their own words, edits it if they like, and posts it straight to the business's real Google listing. No app, no login, no account.

## Where the three reviews agree

All three reviewers landed on the same four points - treat these as settled:

1. **Founder-stage low pricing is correct.** The goal right now is proof of payment, not revenue.
2. **The first 20 customers must be framed as Founding Partners** - never as "I'm cheap because I'm new."
3. **Grandfather the first 20, and raise prices for #21 onward.**
4. **The metric that matters is renewal at month 2** - not signups.

## Where they disagree

| Point | The two 8+/10 reviews | The 5/10 review |
|---|---|---|
| ₹299 / ₹499 gap between tiers | Fine, keep both | Broken - ₹200 apart teaches you nothing |
| "Locked for life" pricing | Recommends it | Dangerous - never promise forever on price |
| ₹299 optics | One flagged "cheap toy" risk | Same, plus: it anchors the whole market low |
| The business model | Strong, 8/10 | Idea 6/10, business 4/10 - doorknocking 250 cafes for ₹1L/mo doesn't scale |

Nobody rated the idea badly. The disagreement is about the business model - single independent cafes vs. multi-outlet chains - not about whether the product works. That split is real and worth holding onto rather than resolving prematurely.

## Final verdict - what we're doing

**Published price list, live on the site today:**

- Basic - ₹499/mo or ₹4,999/yr
- Full - ₹1,499/mo or ₹14,999/yr
- Setup - ₹999 one-time

**How the first 20 are actually sold:**

> **Founding Partner Program** - Full plan at ₹499/month, locked for 12 months. Setup waived.

Basic is not sold at all during validation.

## Why this specific structure

- **₹499 stays, but it now buys Full.** Same number we're comfortable quoting at the door, same easy yes - except the customer is now on the analytics product. That's the only way to find out whether shift view and menu breakdown are actually worth paying for. Selling 20 Basic accounts would finish validation knowing only that ₹299 is cheap.
- **Killing Basic for now removes the ₹200-gap problem** without having to guess a Basic price we can't yet justify. The right Basic price gets learned from the founders who say "I only ever look at the scan count."
- **A published ₹1,499 does work ₹299 never could.** The owner sees they're getting a ₹1,499 product for ₹499 - a real discount, which reads as valuable. ₹299 with nothing above it reads as a toy, a risk flagged independently by two of the three reviews.
- **12 months, not "for life."** "For life" sounds generous and costs nothing today, but three years in we'd still be servicing accounts at ₹499 while costs rise, with no way to fix it without breaking a public promise. 12 months is long enough that every founder feels looked after, and it leaves a clean, non-awkward renewal conversation.
- **Setup waived, not discounted.** A concession that can be given away in the room to close a hesitant owner, at zero real cost.

## Two open questions before this is fully safe

1. **What does one scan actually cost?** A busy cafe doing 400 scans a month, if each is a paid API call, could put ₹499 near or below gross cost. Needs a real number before ₹499 is confirmed safe as a floor.
2. **Test the chain thesis during the same doorknocking round.** Out of the first 20, deliberately land 3-4 owners who run multiple outlets and quote them per-location. If a 5-outlet owner says yes at ₹499 × 5, that's a materially better business than 250 single cafes - and it's the single highest-value thing the first 20 can teach us.

## What changed on the site

- Landing page pricing section now shows the real list prices (Basic ₹499/mo or ₹4,999/yr, Full ₹1,499/mo or ₹14,999/yr, ₹999 one-time setup), with Basic marked "by invite only during launch."
- A **Founding Partner** callout sits above the price cards: Full plan at ₹499/month, locked 12 months, setup waived, limited to the first 20 businesses.
